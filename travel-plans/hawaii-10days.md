# Hong Kong to Hawaii

Nine nights, no driving. Big Island for the crater, Kauai for the 1993 film sites, Waikiki for Pearl Harbor and the Jurassic World sets. Prices are in Hong Kong dollars for one room.

Leave Hong Kong on a Friday in late April or May. Sample week: depart Friday 16 April 2027, land home Tuesday 27 April 2027. The same weekdays start Friday 14 May 2027.

There is no direct flight from Hilo to Lihue and no ferry. Every hop goes through Honolulu. Honolulu is last so that connection is the flight home.

## Schedule

| Friday | Waikiki | Morning flight from Lihue. Drop the bags. Walk Waikiki. Nothing reserved. |
| Saturday | Waikiki | Pearl Harbor in the morning. The USS Arizona Memorial is free and timed; reserve it before leaving home. ʻIolani Palace audio tour in the afternoon. The palace is closed Sunday and Monday. Chinatown for dinner. |
| Sunday | Waikiki | Kualoa Ranch. Waikiki pickup at 7:00 or 8:00 am. The tour is 2.5 hours and runs on Sunday. |
| Monday | Plane | Honolulu to Hong Kong. |

Tuesday morning in Lihue is free. A helicopter landing at Manawaiopuna Falls would fit before Kipu, about HK$3,500 per person. If cloud cancels it, there is no later morning to move it to.

## Getting around

- **Big Island.** Hele-On Route 11 is free through 31 December 2028 and runs seven days. Leave the big suitcase at the Hilo hotel and take a small bag for the crater night. The last bus down is about 6:15 pm.
- **Kauai.** The bus is HK$16 a ride and stops at Lihue Airport. It does not run on Sunday, and it does not enter Kipu or climb to the canyon lookouts.
- **Honolulu.** TheBus caps at HK$59 per person per day with a HOLO card. Stay in Waikiki so the Pearl Harbor bus and the Kualoa van both start at the hotel.

## Book in this order

1. US visitor visa, if travelling on an HKSAR passport. The Guam waiver does not cover Hawaii. A full British citizen passport can use ESTA instead.
2. Volcano House for the Sunday night. There are 33 rooms.
3. Wednesday movie tour, Tuesday Kipu, Thursday canyon tour.
4. Saturday Pearl Harbor time on Recreation.gov, and the Saturday palace audio tour. No same-day palace tickets.
5. Open-jaw ticket into Hilo and home from Honolulu, then the two short hops. Compare Hawaiian and Southwest with bag fees included.
6. Sunday Kualoa tour with the Waikiki pickup.

No bundled pass beats booking this directly. There is no island-hopper flight ticket. Movie, Kipu, and the canyon are three separate companies.
